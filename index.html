BRIGADEIRO

A traditional Brazilian dessert, the perfect sweet treat.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons of unsalted butter
  • 2.1 oz of 50% cocoa powder
  • 2.1 oz of chocolate
  • 17.5 oz of condensed milk
  • 7.0 oz of heavy cream
  • Chocolate sprinklers

Instructions

  1. Add the butter to your pan and let it melt in low heat
  2. Turn off the stove
  3. Add the cocoa powder, the chocolate, the heavy cream and the condensed milk
  4. Mix it before turning up the heat
  5. After everything is mixed, turn up the stove to medium heat
  6. Stir constantly with a spatula, until it starts to thicken up
  7. Keep stirring until the mixture thickens and pulls away from the bottom of the pan
  8. Turn off the heat, and keep stirring for a couple minutes
  9. With caution, put the mixture in a bowl
  10. Let it cool in the fridge for at least 4 hours
  11. Grease your hands with a little butter
  12. Roll small portions into balls
  13. Coat them with chocolate sprinklers
